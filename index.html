German Publish

German Publish is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel German Steam (@german_steamtrade) to upload the owner's own short videos to the same channel on a schedule.

The tool is not offered to other users. It only uploads videos created by the channel owner and reads basic information about the owner's channel.

YouTube API Services

German Publish uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to:

  • upload the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel (videos.insert);
  • confirm which channel is authorized (channels.list, mine=true);
  • post one comment from the channel under the owner's own video (commentThreads.insert).

By using German Publish you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and grants access to the channel.
  2. The tool uploads a finished video with its title and description.
  3. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
